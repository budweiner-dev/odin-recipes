Chicken Soup

Time to make ramen in a ladle and pretend it is chicken noodle soup

Ingredients

  • Maruchan Ramen
  • Water
  • Mixed Veggies-Frozen

Steps

  • Boil noodles in water, strain
  • Add hot water to a bowl and follow with noodles
  • sprinkle veggies ontop
  • Let the veggies defrost and cool down your hot ass ramen
