Egg Bites

Today you are going to learn how to make a easy high protein egg bites

Ingredients

  • Cheese
  • Cottage Cheese
  • Egg Whites
  • Diced onion

Steps

  • Mix eggs, cottage cheese, and onion in a large bowl
  • Salt, pepper, and season as you wish
  • sprinkle cheese ontop
  • cook at 450 degrees for an hour
